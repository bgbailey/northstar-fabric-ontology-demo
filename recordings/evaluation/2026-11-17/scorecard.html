Limitations

  • Results reflect the synthetic Northstar Wireless service assurance scenario in the recorded demo tenant configuration (model deployment, capacity size, and region).
  • A statement of accuracy benefit is supported only where the 95% paired bootstrap interval excludes zero; ties and inconclusive intervals are published alongside wins.
  • Workspace, tenant, and item UUIDs are omitted from published scorecards.
